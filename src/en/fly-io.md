# Fly.io

[Fly.io](https://fly.io/) is a new public cloud.

I worked for Fly.io from March 2023 to October 2025. It was a wild ride. While I wasn't affected by [their 2024 layoff](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41441218), I quit about a year later.
