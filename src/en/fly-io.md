# Fly.io

Fly.io is the company behind [Sprites](https://fly.io/sprites). It was more like [Heroku](https://www.heroku.com/) before the AI era.

I worked for Fly.io from March 2023 to October 2025. This was the first time working for [a YC-backed startup](https://www.ycombinator.com/companies/fly-io) and it was wild. While I wasn't affected by [their 2024 layoff](https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=41441218), I quit about a year later.
