# Amazon

Random bits I know about Amazon. I worked there for 10 years!

## Security, durability, availability, speed

[Colm tweeted](https://x.com/colmmacc/status/986286693572493312?lang=en) in 2018;

> Security, durability, availability, speed.
> That's the priority order I think about for design trade-offs and it's never let me down.
> Simplicity and Cost are huge too but more like guiding principles.

That's not Amazon's "official" priority order, but it seems overall good trade-offs.
