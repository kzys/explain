# Postgres

## There should be no more than 50% of the membership of the Core Team working for the same company

[Statement from the PostgreSQL Core Team on the EDB acquisition of 2ndQuadrant](https://www.postgresql.org/about/news/statement-from-the-postgresql-core-team-on-the-edb-acquisition-of-2ndquadrant-2094/)

> There has long been an unwritten rule that there should be no more than 50% of the membership of the Core Team working for the same company. As a result, there has been a lively ongoing discussion within the core team on how to best resolve this issue. For obvious reasons we do not want to rush into making changes.

The situation was addressed by [adding two additional members to the Core Team](https://www.postgresql.org/about/news/new-postgresql-core-team-members-2103/).
