# Blaugust

[Blaugust](https://www.containsmoderateperil.com/blog/2026/7/14/blaugust-2026-q-ampa) is a month-long blogging event:

> It’s month long event to encourage new bloggers and content creators, as well as those who’ve drifted away from existing blogs who are looking for the motivation to resume writing.

Apparently [it was running since 2014](https://aggronaut.com/2014/07/30/gospel-of-blaugust/):

> Thanks to cramming together of blog and august, Blaugust was born, and the idea is to take the challenge during the month of August to write a new post every day.

but I know that in 2026, because of [its Japanese counterpart](https://www.nejimakiblog.com/entry/blaugust-2026-blog-write-hachigatsu). I knew [100 Days To Offload](https://100daystooffload.com/) though.
