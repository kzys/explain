# I broke my wrist

In July 2026, I broke my right wrist, had surgery and got a plate in the wrist.

## 07/09

I fell off my bike and broke my right wrist. At that time, I thought that I sprained the wrist, took pain killers and slept.

## 07/10

I went to a nearby urgent care to take an X-ray, but the doctor told me that my bone was broken and had to go to ER instead. So I went to ER, got a splint and had a referral for a specialist visit.

## 07/16

I had a specialist visit and the doctor told me that I should have surgery.

## 07/21

I had surgery. It went well. Well, actually, I was on anesthesia and didn't remember anything during the surgery itself.
