# Tufty 2350

[Tufty 2350](https://shop.pimoroni.com/products/tufty-2350?variant=55811986194811) is a badge device by Pimoroni. I bought the device [from Adafruit](https://www.adafruit.com/product/6463) in 2026.

## What are badge devices?

Hackaday made a documentary titled [BadgeLife](https://hackaday.com/2018/08/20/badgelife-the-hardware-demoscene/) in 2018:

> Last week, tens of thousands of people headed home from Vegas, fresh out of this year’s DEF CON. This was a great year for DEF CON, especially when it comes to hardware. This was the year independent badges took over, thanks to a small community of people dedicated to creating small-run hardware, puzzles, and PCB art for thousands of conference-goers. This is badgelife, a demoscene of hardware, and this is just the beginning. It’s only going to get bigger from here on out.

In addition to independently-made badges, [DEF CON has made and handed out official badge devices for years](https://www.defcon.org/html/links/dc-badge.html). Then Pimoroni made [Badger 2040](https://shop.pimoroni.com/en-us/products/badger-2040) in 2022:

> In honour of Raspberry Pi's 10th birthday, we've fused a RP2040 microcontroller with an EPD display to produce a stylishly monochrome, maker friendly, e-paper badge(r) to attach to your person, your office door or to prop up on your desk.

[Tufty 2040](https://shop.pimoroni.com/en-us/products/tufty-2040) is Badger's LCD variant and Tufty 2350 is its successor.

GitHub handed out custom versions of the Pimoroni badges during their annual conferences in
[2023](https://www.raspberrypi.com/news/github-universe-attendees-treated-to-custom-rp2040-badgers/),
[2024](https://github.blog/news-insights/company-news/career-growth-learning-and-fun-oh-my-your-guide-to-github-universe-2024/#hack-your-badge-station) and
[2025](https://simonwillison.net/2025/Oct/28/github-universe-badge/).
