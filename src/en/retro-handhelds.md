# Retro Handhelds

Retro handhelds are small devices that are designed to run emulators. They often run Linux or Android, and some devices (such as the [Anbernic RG353V](https://anbernic.com/products/rg353v-rg353vs)) even support both. [Retro Catalog](https://retrocatalog.com/) is good for comparing the devices. [Rocknix](https://rocknix.org/) is one of the Linux distributions for such devices.

I've been down the small Internet rabbit hole for the past few days.

While I'm not really into gaming, to be honest, they would work as a good starting point for building [writerDeck](https://www.writerdeck.org/), [cyberDeck](https://www.reddit.com/r/cyberDeck/) and [digital audio players](https://www.reddit.com/r/ConsolesAsDAPs/).

I know there are "for hackers" devices such as
[CardputerZero](https://shop.m5stack.com/pages/m5-cardputerzero),
[uConsole](https://www.clockworkpi.com/home-uconsole) and
[PocketTerm](https://www.waveshare.com/pocketterm35.htm). Retro handhelds cover a much wider price range, and some are under $100.
